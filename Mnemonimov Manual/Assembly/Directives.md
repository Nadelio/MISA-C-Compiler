# Directives

## Commands to the Assembler
The MISA Assembler features **directives** that, among other things, help you structure your program. Directives are commands to the assembler that affect how it generates bytecode.

It is important to know that a directive is a different concept from an instruction. A directive is an assemble-time statement, that is, it only takes effect when the assembler is converting your source code into bytecode. This means that directives are not executed, as they are not instructions and do not exist at runtime.
